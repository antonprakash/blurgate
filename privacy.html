Privacy Policy

We collect information about what you want to blur, how blurry you want it, and your preferred level of scandal concealment. We also track how many times you've clicked on mysterious keywords (for scientific purposes, obviously).

Your data helps us improve our blurring algorithms and determine which scandals deserve the coveted "-gate" suffix. We may also use it to send you updates about new blur technologies and trending scandals.

Data Protection

Your personal information is protected with military-grade blurring technology. Even we can't see it clearly, which makes customer service interesting but ensures maximum privacy.

Third-Party Sharing

We don't share your data with third parties, mainly because it's too blurry for anyone else to read. Your secrets are safe with us and our advanced blur protection systems.
